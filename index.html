About me

I build interfaces that feel like they have weight, light and motion — not just pixels.

I'm Alex, a creative developer and 3D motion designer based between the browser and the render queue. For the last seven years I've been fusing front-end engineering with real-time 3D, motion design and sound to build products people remember using.

My work sits at the intersection of design systems and WebGL — brand sites, product launches and interactive experiments for studios and startups who want their site to feel alive, not templated.
